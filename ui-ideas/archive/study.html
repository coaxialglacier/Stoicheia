Each box is an idea, and a line goes from an idea down to an idea it is made of. The numbers are the order you learn them in: the parts first, then what they make. A teal box is an atom. A dashed box is an idea you know: it has no number, and you can still click it to open its page.

Graph and Page, for any idea. This is the layout of Graph and Page, built from what Expand to Parts knows about each idea: what it is, and what it is made of.

Its other answers are not written yet. Each idea with an entry in the concept library, such as Captured variables, has its full study in the original Graph and Page.
