Expand to Parts. A whole idea on the left: a chunk. Click it, and what it is made of opens to its right. Keep clicking until you reach the atoms: parts that cannot be split any further.

Each idea appears once, in the first column that shows it. When another idea is also made of it, a dashed line wires back to that same pill, and the pill shows how many open ideas use it. Point at a pill to light its lines.
